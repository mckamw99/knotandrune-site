About

A small press with a long view

Knot & Rune makes coloring books and keepsakes from the places where Celtic, Norse and Highland worlds meet.

How we work

Single-theme shelves are crowded. We make books about the crossovers: Norse runes in Gaelic Scotland, trows and trolls, knotwork shared by Pictish stones and runestones.

Every design comes with a short note of real history or lore, checked against published sources. When a tradition is newer than it looks, we say so. When we can't source a claim, we leave it out.

Our designs are drawn with digital tools, including AI image tools, and edited by hand. Every book says so on its copyright page.

Shops, clan societies and Highland games

We'd like to work with clan societies, Highland games vendors and independent shops. Wholesale and partnership details will be posted here before our first book launches.
