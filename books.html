The Celtic Tree Zodiac

A Coloring Book of the Thirteen Tree Months · W. Kroboth

Thirteen trees. One alphabet cut in stone. A poet's calendar of the year.

For each of the thirteen tree months (birch, rowan, ash, alder, willow, hawthorn, oak, holly, hazel, vine, ivy, reed and elder) there is a full-page tree portrait and a knotwork sign medallion with its Ogham letter and a two-sentence note on the tree's lore and natural history.

  • 31 single-sided designs on 8.5 × 11 in pages, so markers won't ruin the next page
  • A thirteen-tree mandala, a wheel of the tree year and the full Ogham alphabet
  • "The Day Between" and a page to write your name in Ogham
  • A short, plain introduction to where the tree calendar comes from, with a source list

Lore and natural history only: no horoscopes. Every claim in the captions comes from a published source, listed at the back of the book.

How we make our books: designs are drawn with digital tools, including AI image tools, and edited by hand. Each book says so on its copyright page.
