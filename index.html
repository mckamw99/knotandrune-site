The Celtic Tree Zodiac

A Coloring Book of the Thirteen Tree Months

Color your way through the tree year, from birch at midwinter to oak at midsummer and elder as the year turns. Each of the thirteen tree months has a full-page botanical portrait and a sign medallion with its Ogham letter, plus a short note on the tree's folklore and natural history.

  • 31 single-sided designs, so markers won't ruin the next page
  • A wheel of the tree year and the full Ogham alphabet
  • A page to write your name in Ogham
  • The honest story of where the tree calendar comes from

The thirteen-month tree calendar was devised by the poet Robert Graves in 1948. Ogham itself is far older and real, carved on stones from about the 4th to 6th centuries. We tell you which is which.
